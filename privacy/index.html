Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Nuvlo Studio LLC ("Nuvlo Studio", "we", "us") handles when you use the PunchedOut mobile app ("PunchedOut" or "the app") and this website, and what choices you have.

At a glance

  • PunchedOut turns a photo of your handwritten timesheet notes into an editable task list, then prepares an email in your mail app for you to send.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers in the app.
  • Your timesheet photo is used only to complete the request you make. Our servers do not save it. Your timesheet emails are sent from your own mail app, not through our servers.
  • Your settings and timesheet history are stored on your device. You can delete them in the app at any time.

Information we handle

Timesheet photos

When you photograph your notes or choose a picture from your library, the app sends it over an encrypted connection to our server. Our server passes it to Anthropic, which provides the Claude AI service, to read the handwriting and return a list of tasks, hours, and days. We do not save the photo on our servers. In the app, the photo is held in memory only while you review the results.

Tasks and timesheet content

The task descriptions, hours, days, notes, and week that you review and edit. After you send a timesheet, a record of it is saved in the history on your device, which keeps up to 200 entries.

Email details

When you are ready to send a timesheet, the app opens your device's mail app with the recipient, subject, signature, and timesheet already filled in. You review the message and send it yourself from your own account. The message does not pass through our servers, and we do not receive your email address, the recipient's address, your signature, or the message.

Subscription and access

Subscriptions are bought and billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, and we do not see your payment details. When you use a feature that requires a subscription, the app sends proof of your purchase so our server can confirm that your subscription is active.

Technical information

Our hosting providers may process technical data such as your IP address and the time of a request in order to deliver the service. We apply rate limits to prevent abuse.

Messages to us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run PunchedOut:

  • Anthropic reads timesheet photos to extract tasks. It handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google process your subscription purchase through the App Store or Google Play, under their own privacy policies.
  • Supabase hosts the database we use to confirm subscription status.
  • Cloud hosting providers run our server.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and our service.

What stays on your device

Your recipient address, signature, and other settings, along with your timesheet history, are stored on your device in the app's local storage.

In Settings you can choose "Clear the Ledger" to delete your history and clear your saved settings. Uninstalling the app also removes this data. Emails you have already sent remain in your mail account and in the recipient's mailbox, and we cannot delete them.

Device permissions

The app asks for camera access so you can photograph your timesheet notes, and for photo library access so you can choose an existing picture. It does not use your location, your contacts, or your microphone.

How long we keep information

  • Photos: not kept on our servers once your request finishes.
  • Subscription status records: for as long as needed to confirm your access.
  • Messages to us: for as long as needed to help you and to meet legal obligations.
  • Provider logs: according to each provider's normal schedule.

Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, subscription checks, and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure.

Your choices and rights

You control the information on your device and can delete it in the app at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Because our servers keep very little, there is often nothing to delete, but you can email us and we will respond to any request we receive.

Children

PunchedOut is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or on this website.

Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy can be sent to support@nuvlostudio.com.
